## Next and previous

| To | Press | Or click |
|---|---|---|
| Show the next slide | <kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>→</kbd>, <kbd>↓</kbd> or <kbd>Page Down</kbd> | **NEXT** |
| Show the previous slide | <kbd>←</kbd>, <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>Page Up</kbd> | **PREV** |
| Show the first slide | <kbd>Home</kbd> | |
| Show the last slide | <kbd>End</kbd> | |

- Navigation stops at the start and end of the presentation.
- Skipped slides are passed over.
- Each keypress moves one slide. Holding the key down doesn’t advance through more slides.
- <kbd>Space</kbd> always means **Next** in Present, even after you click a button on the right.
- These keys don't act while you're typing in a field, such as the message composer.

The counter beside **NEXT** shows where you are, for example "4 / 25". It counts only slides that aren't skipped.

Before going live, the same keys choose the slide the service will start on, marked **Starts here**. Nothing is shown on the audience screens until you go live.

## Jump to a slide

- **Click** any slide in the slide list to show it, with the normal transition. This works for skipped slides too.
- **Click a section** in the Sections strip to jump to it.
- **Type a slide number**, then press <kbd>Return</kbd>. A preview of that slide appears over the Program monitor while you type, and nothing changes on screen until you press <kbd>Return</kbd>. Press <kbd>Delete</kbd> to remove a digit, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. You can also choose **Presentation › Go to Slide…**.

Slide numbers match the slide list, including skipped slides.

## Presenter clickers

Most presenter clickers work without any setup, including Logitech, Kensington and Targus remotes.

| Clicker button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Next / Forward | Next slide |
| Back | Previous slide |
| Black screen | Blank (while live) |
| Start slideshow | Go Live; pressing it again ends live |

## Navigating from other workspaces

While live, the [live controls](/present/live-changes/#the-live-controls) in Design and Library have **Prev** and **Next** buttons. The keyboard navigation keys work only in Present, so arrow keys keep their usual meaning while you edit.

## Main passage

When a sermon keeps returning to one passage, you can mark it as the **main passage**. Right-click its group in Design and choose **Set as Main Passage**.

While live, when you've moved away from the main passage to show another verse, press <kbd>R</kbd> (**Back to Main Passage**) to go straight back to where you left it. The slide list marks the slide to return to with **Return here**, and the status bar shows, for example, “Romans 8:31 · main passage”.

While a main passage is set, a **Passages** strip under the monitors lists it, with the verses you've shown from other passages under **Other passages**.
