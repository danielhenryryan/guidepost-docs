Present (<kbd>⌘2</kbd>) is where you run the service. Controls stay in fixed positions. Amber marks what’s live or active.

## Status bar

Across the top:

- **READY** before the service, or **LIVE** and the time since you went live.
- The name of what's on screen, such as "Amazing Grace · Chorus".
- Badges for anything active: **BLANK**, **LOGO**, **FROZEN**, **CLEAR TEXT**, **MESSAGE** or **COUNTDOWN**.
- A summary of your screens, such as "Epson Projector · Sanctuary TV (AirPlay) · Stage Display". Click it to open the [Screens panel](/screens/screens-panel/).
- The number of [network viewers](/screens/network-viewers/) watching.

## Slide list

On the left, the same slides and groups as in Design. Click a slide to show it. Badges mark:

- **LIVE**: the slide the audience sees.
- **Up next**: the slide **Next** will show.
- **Starts here**: before going live, the slide the service will start on.
- **Skipped**: slides that **Next** passes over.

## Monitors

In the middle:

- **Program** shows exactly what the audience screens show, including Blank, Logo, Freeze and Clear text, the message, the countdown, transitions and video.
- **Up Next** shows the next slide and its **Notes**.
- **Stage Display** shows a live view of the Stage Display, when one is in use. Otherwise this monitor shows only a slide number and the slide after Up Next.

To make a different monitor the largest, hover over it and click the expand button. Guidepost remembers your choice.

## Sections and passages

When the live slide is in a song, a **Sections** strip appears under the monitors. It lists the song's sections in order and highlights the current one. Click a section to jump to it, or use **Repeat** and **Skip**. While a [main passage](/present/navigation/#main-passage) is set, a **Passages** strip appears in the same place. See [Changing the service while live](/present/live-changes/#repeat-or-skip-a-song-section).

## Controls on the right

On the right, top to bottom:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Timers** | Before the service, the [countdown](/present/timers/). While live, the elapsed time and **End Live**. Below that, the [speaker timer](/present/timers/#speaker-timer). |
| **Stage Display** | [Stage messages](/present/cues/) for the speaker. Shown only while a Stage Display is connected. |
| **Scripture** and **Song** | [Add content](/present/live-changes/) during the service. |
| **Message** | Compose and show a [message](/present/messages/). |
| **Video** | Play, pause and scrub [video](/present/video/). Shown only when the live slide has video. |
| **Blank**, **Logo**, **Freeze**, **Clear** | [Blank, Logo, Freeze and Clear text](/present/holds/). |
| **GO LIVE**, then **NEXT** and **PREV** | Start the service, then move through it. The counter shows where you are, such as "4 / 25". |

These controls appear and disappear in fixed positions, so the buttons you use most never move. Clicking one never takes keyboard focus, so <kbd>Space</kbd> always means **Next**.
