## Before you go live

At the bottom of the control rack, under the **GO LIVE** button, the readiness line shows where the slides will go and which slide the service starts on, for example:

> → Epson Projector + Sanctuary TV (AirPlay) + Stage Display + 3 viewers · starts at slide 1

If something needs attention, it's listed below, with a link to fix it:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **No audience screen selected** | Open the Screens panel and turn on a screen. See [The Screens panel](/screens/screens-panel/). |
| **Sanctuary TV (AirPlay) not connected** | Click **Show me how** and connect the TV from Control Center. See [AirPlay TVs](/screens/airplay/). |
| **Sanctuary TV is mirroring your screen** | Click **Fix** and switch the TV to an extended display. |
| **2 missing media items** | Replace the missing files in Design. See [Missing media](/design/media/#missing-media). |
| **License required** | See [Licensing and the beta](/reference/licensing/). |

Before going live you can click slides, or use the navigation keys, to choose where the service starts. Nothing appears on the audience screens until you go live. The slide marked **Starts here** is the one that will be shown first.

## Go live

Click **GO LIVE** or press <kbd>⌘Return</kbd>. <kbd>⌘Return</kbd> works from any workspace. A presenter clicker's "start slideshow" button also goes live.

The audience screens open and show the starting slide with the theme's transition. The rack's big button becomes **NEXT**.

### If Guidepost asks a question first

You may see one of these prompts before going live:

- **This presentation has no slides** (or **Every slide is skipped**): click **Go to Design** to add or turn on slides.
- **No audience screen**: nothing is set to show the slides. The dialog lists each screen's status. Click **Open Screens** to open the Screens panel, or **Go Live anyway** to go live for the Stage Display and network viewers only. Select **Don't ask again** to skip this question in future. You can turn it back on in **Settings › Screens**.
- **Only your screen is connected**: choose **Use a windowed audience screen** to show the slides in a resizable window, or **Network viewers only**. The choice lasts until you quit Guidepost.
- **Media items are missing**: the dialog lists each missing file and where it's used. Click **Fix now** to go to the first affected slide, or **Go Live anyway**.

## While live

- Guidepost keeps your Mac awake. You can turn this off in **Settings › General**.
- Guidepost can also silence notifications during the service. See [Silencing notifications](/reference/mac-integration/#silencing-notifications-while-live).
- If you switch to Design or Library, the [Live bar](/present/live-changes/#the-live-bar) keeps the service in view.
- Opening, closing or creating a presentation, or quitting Guidepost, first asks whether to end the service.
- App updates never install or ask for attention while you're live.

## End live

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> in Present, click **End Live** in the Timers module, or choose **Presentation › End Live**. Confirm **End Live**.

When the service ends:

- the audience screens close;
- the Stage Display and network viewers return to the idle screen;
- any hold, message or cue is cleared, as are this run's song repeats and skips;
- the presentation stays open, and the slide you were on is marked **Starts here**.
