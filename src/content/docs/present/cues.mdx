A **stage message** is a short private message for the speaker or the band. It appears as a large amber banner across the bottom of the [Stage Display](/screens/stage-display/), and never on the audience screens.

The **Stage Display** controls appear on the right of Present while a Stage Display is connected.

## Send a stage message

1. Click **Send stage message**, or press <kbd>⇧⌘C</kbd>.
2. Click a chip to send it right away:
   - **5 min**, **2 min**, **1 min**, **Wrap up**
   - **Slow down**, **Speak up**, **Stand by**, **Take a sip**
   - any stage messages you’ve added yourself
3. Or type your own in the **Stage message** field and click **Send**. A stage message can be up to 120 characters.

Choose how long it stays up: **5 s**, **15 s** or **Until cleared**. Guidepost remembers your choice.

After sending, a notice confirms **Stage message sent** with **Undo**, and the controls show the active stage message with the time remaining and **Clear**.

## Add your own stage messages

Click **+ Add** under the chips, type the stage message, and press <kbd>Return</kbd>. Right-click one of your stage messages to remove it. You can also manage them in **Settings › Stage Display › Your stage messages**.

## Confirm before sending

To review a stage message before sending it, turn on **Confirm before sending a stage message** in **Settings › General**. Clicking a chip will then fill in the text. Click **Send** to send it.
