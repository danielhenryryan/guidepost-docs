The four **holds** change what the audience screens show for a moment, then return to the slide. They're the pedals above **NEXT** on the control rack.

| Hold | What the audience sees | Key |
|---|---|---|
| **Blank** | Pure black. Nothing else. | <kbd>B</kbd> |
| **Logo** | Your church logo on its background color. | <kbd>L</kbd> |
| **Freeze** | The current slide stays up while you move ahead privately. | <kbd>F</kbd> |
| **Clear** text | The slide's background only, with text and layers hidden. | <kbd>C</kbd> |

- Only one hold is active at a time. Choosing another replaces it.
- Press the active hold again, or click its pedal, to return to the slide.
- Holds apply to every audience screen and to network viewers.
- Moving to another slide doesn't end a hold. Use it to line up the next slide while the screen is blank.
- Holds are available while live. The status bar shows a badge for the active hold, such as **BLANK**.

## Blank from anywhere

**Blank** also has a shortcut that works in every workspace while live: <kbd>⇧⌘B</kbd>. It's also on the [Live bar](/present/live-changes/#the-live-bar). The other hold keys work only in Present.

## Logo

Logo needs a logo image. Add one in **Settings › Screens › Logo and idle screen**: click **Choose…**, pick an image, and choose a **Logo background color**. Until a logo is set, the Logo pedal is unavailable; clicking it opens that setting.

## Freeze

Freeze is for fixing something without the audience seeing it. While frozen:

- the audience screens keep showing the frozen slide;
- you can move through slides, or edit in Design, privately;
- the Program monitor keeps showing the frozen slide with a dashed amber outline, and a small inset shows where you actually are;
- the Stage Display follows you, not the frozen slide, so the band can see what's coming.

When you leave Freeze, the audience screens fade to the slide you're on.

## Holds and the message

A [message](/present/messages/) stays visible over Freeze and Clear text, but is hidden under Blank and Logo, so Blank always means a black screen.
