## Countdown

### Start a countdown

1. Before going live, click the time in the **Countdown** row and type a length, such as `10`, `10:00` or `90s`.

   To count to a time of day instead, click **Count to** and type the time, such as `10:30 AM`.

2. Choose what happens **At zero**:
   - **Go Live**: the service goes live automatically on the starting slide.
   - **Hold at 0:00**: the countdown stays at zero until you go live yourself.
   - **Show Logo**: the screen switches to your logo.
3. Click **Start**.

Starting a countdown opens the audience screens and shows the countdown in the presentation's theme. The status bar shows **COUNTDOWN** and the time the service starts. Click **Stop** to cancel it.

The choices you make there apply to today only. The defaults, and the countdown's colors and background, are in **Settings › Countdown**.

### If something isn't ready at zero

When the countdown reaches zero, Guidepost runs the same checks as the **GO LIVE** button. If one of them would need an answer, such as "No audience screen selected", the countdown stays at 0:00 and tells you what needs attention. The audience screens continue showing the countdown.

### Announcement loop

The countdown can rotate your [saved slides](/design/saved-slides/), such as weekly announcements, beside the time.

- Click the **Announcement loop** line under the countdown to turn the loop on, choose how often the slide changes (5, 8, 12 or 20 seconds), and pick the saved slides to show.
- To set the slides that are used every week, go to **Settings › Countdown › Announcement loop**.

### Messages during the countdown

You can show a [message](/present/messages/) over the countdown. It's hidden automatically when the service goes live.

## Speaker timer

The speaker timer appears only on the Stage Display, so the speaker can see how much time is left without the congregation seeing it.

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Set the time | Click a preset, such as **Sermon 25:00**, or click the time and type one. |
| Start or pause | Click **Start** or **Pause**. |
| Add or remove time | Click **+30** or **−30**. |
| Reset | Click **Reset**, or right-click the timer and choose **Reset Speaker Timer**. |

On the Stage Display, the timer changes color when paused and flashes when it reaches zero. To let it count past zero into overtime (shown in red), turn on **Speaker timer counts past zero** in **Settings › Stage Display**. That page is also where you add your own presets, such as "Announcements 3:00".

The speaker timer keeps running if you end live, so you can reset it when you're ready.

## Elapsed time

Once live, the Timers controls show **LIVE**, the time you went live, and how long the service has been running. The same elapsed time is in the status bar.
