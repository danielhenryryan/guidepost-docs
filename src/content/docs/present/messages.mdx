A **message** is a short line of text shown over the slides along the bottom of the audience screens, for example "Kids, head to Children's Church" or "Please silence your phones". The slide underneath stays as it is.

## Show a message

1. Press <kbd>M</kbd>, or click **Message** on the control rack. The composer opens with the cursor in the text field.
2. Type the message, or click a saved message to fill it in.
3. Choose a style:
   - **Banner**: a full-width band across the bottom.
   - **Bar**: a slimmer strip.
   - **Corner**: a small badge in the corner.
4. To hide it automatically, type a number of seconds in the auto-hide field.
5. Click **Show**.

Pressing <kbd>M</kbd> only opens the composer. A message appears on screen only when you click **Show**, so a stray key never puts text in front of the congregation.

While a message is up, the status bar shows a **MESSAGE** badge, and the composer shows its status, such as "showing · hides in 0:12".

## Change or hide it

- Edit the text and click **Update text** to change the message without hiding it.
- Click **Hide** to remove it.

Messages can be shown while live or during the countdown. Ending live clears any message.

## Saved messages

Messages you use often can be saved as chips above the text field. Guidepost starts with "Kids to Children's Church", "Silence phones" and "Welcome visitors".

- Click a chip to fill the composer with its text. It doesn't show it.
- To save the current text, click **+ Save**.
- Right-click a chip to **Rename…** or **Delete** it.

## Where messages appear

The message is shown over the slide, Freeze, Clear text and the countdown, on every audience screen and on network viewers. It's hidden while the screen is on Blank or Logo, and the composer says so ("hidden by Blank"). It reappears when the hold ends.

To send a private note to the speaker instead, use a [cue](/present/cues/).
