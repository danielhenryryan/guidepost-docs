The Stage Display is a confidence monitor for the people on stage. It shows what's on screen now, what's coming next, and private information the congregation doesn't see.

To set one up, open the [Screens panel](/screens/screens-panel/) and choose a display, or a window, under **Stage Display**. An iPad connected with Sidecar works as a Stage Display too.

## What it shows

- **Clock**, top left.
- What's on screen, such as "Amazing Grace · Chorus", at the top.
- **Speaker timer**, top right. See [Speaker timer](/present/timers/#speaker-timer).
- **Now**: the current lyrics or verse, in large type. Chords appear above the lyrics when turned on.
- **Next**: the next section and its first line, or a thumbnail of the next slide.
- **Notes**: the slide's notes.
- **Song map**: the song's sections in order, with the current one underlined and skipped sections struck through.
- **Cue banner**: an amber banner across the bottom when you send a [cue](/present/cues/).

Before you go live, the Stage Display shows an idle screen, or the countdown when one is running.

## When the audience screen changes

The Stage Display always follows where you are, so the band never loses their place:

| Audience sees | Stage Display shows |
|---|---|
| **Blank** | **AUDIENCE BLANKED**, with the lyrics still visible. Or, if you choose, a blank stage too. |
| **Logo** | **AUDIENCE: LOGO**, with the lyrics still visible. |
| **Freeze** | **AUDIENCE FROZEN**, and the slide you've moved to, not the frozen one. |

## Chords

Chords come from the song's lyrics, where they're written in square brackets, such as `[G]Amazing [C]grace`. They never appear on audience screens. To show them on the Stage Display, turn on **Chords above lyrics** in **Settings › Stage Display**. See [Songs and the song editor](/library/songs/#chords).

## Settings

**Settings › Stage Display** controls what the Stage Display shows, with a live preview:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Shown on the stage** | Next, Song map, Clock, Slide notes, Speaker timer, Chords above lyrics |
| **Next shows** | Text or Thumbnail |
| **Clock** | 12-hour or 24-hour |
| **Lyric size** | Normal, Large or Extra large |
| **Background** | Any color |
| **When the audience screen is blanked** | Show a notice, or Blank the stage too |
| **Speaker timer counts past zero** | On or off |
| **Your cues** | Your own cue chips |
| **Timer presets** | Speaker timer presets, such as Sermon 25:00 |

## In Present

When a Stage Display is active, the third monitor in Present shows a live view of it, and the control rack shows the **Stage Display** module for sending cues.
