---
title: What Guidepost sends
description: What leaves your Mac, when, and how to turn it off.
---

Guidepost works offline. Your presentations, songs, scripture, media and Library stay on your Mac. A few things are sent over the internet, and this page lists them. The [privacy policy](https://guidepostpresenter.com/privacy/) has the full details.

## Anonymous usage data

Guidepost sends anonymous usage data so we can see which features churches use and how often something goes wrong. It's on by default.

**What's sent:** which features are used, with counts and times in coarse ranges. For example: a presentation was opened (with about how many slides), a song or passage was added, a service went live and ended (roughly how long it ran and how many slides were shown), or a video wouldn't open. A service is summarized once, after you end live, not slide by slide. Each event includes the Guidepost and macOS versions, your Mac's model, and your language setting.

**What's never sent:** your content (song titles, lyrics, scripture, slide text, file and theme names), your church's name or anyone's name, email addresses, license keys, or anything that identifies you or your Mac.

To turn it off, open **Settings › General** and turn off **Share anonymous usage data**. It takes effect right away, though anything recorded in the last minute may still be sent.

Usage data never delays going live. If your Mac is offline, anything recorded is sent once it's back online, or lost if Guidepost quits first.

[More about usage data](https://guidepostpresenter.com/privacy/#usage-data)

## License activation

When you click **Activate**, Guidepost sends your license key, a code made from your Mac's hardware ID (not the ID itself), and your Mac's name, so the license can count your Macs. It checks the license again when you click **Refresh license**, and on its own now and then when you're online. **Deactivate this computer** removes the Mac from your license.

The Mac App Store version doesn't activate a license.

[More about licensing](https://guidepostpresenter.com/privacy/#licensing)

## Report a Problem and Send Feedback

Nothing is sent until you click **Send report** or **Send feedback**. A report includes what you typed, your email if you add one, and, if **Include diagnostic details** is selected, the details shown in the sheet: the Guidepost and macOS versions, your Mac model, your display setup, and Guidepost's activity log from the last 30 minutes. Your Mac's name, your account name and your home folder are removed from the details before they're shown. See [Getting help](/reference/support/).

[More about reports](https://guidepostpresenter.com/privacy/#reports)

## Update checks

When **Check for updates automatically** is on in **Settings › General**, Guidepost checks for a new version about once a day. The check is a plain download of the list of versions and sends nothing about you. Turn the setting off to check only when you choose **Guidepost › Check for Updates…**. See [Updates](/reference/updates/).

The Mac App Store version is updated by the App Store instead.

## Network viewers

[Network viewers](/screens/network-viewers/) connect straight to your Mac over the church network. The slides never go through the internet.
