Follow these steps to make a presentation with a welcome slide, a song and a Bible passage, then show it on a screen. The links in each section have more detail.

<Aside type="tip">
To see a finished service first, choose **Help › Try the Sample Service**. It opens a copy of a Sunday service that you can edit and run.
</Aside>

## 1. Create a presentation

<Steps>

1. On the Start screen, click **New Presentation**, or choose **File › New** (<kbd>⌘N</kbd>).

   The presentation opens in Design, empty and named **Untitled**.

2. Choose **File › Save** (<kbd>⌘S</kbd>), name the file (for example, "Sunday, Nov 2"), and choose where to keep it.

   The presentation's name is always its file name. Guidepost autosaves a working copy as you work, but only **Save** updates your file. See [Saving and recovery](/files/saving/).

</Steps>

## 2. Add a welcome slide

<Steps>

1. In the **+ Add slide** row above the slide list, click **Text**.

   A Text slide is added, ready for you to type its heading on the canvas.

2. Type a heading, such as "Welcome". To add a second line, such as your church name and service time, type it in the **Body** field in the inspector on the right.

</Steps>

To use a different layout, click the small arrow on the **Text** card and choose **Title**, **Body**, **Quote**, **Lower third** or **Announcement**. See [Adding slides](/design/adding-slides/).

## 3. Add a song

<Steps>

1. Click **Song** in the **+ Add slide** row (<kbd>⇧⌘W</kbd>).

2. Type part of the title, an author or a line of lyrics, then choose the song.

   Guidepost includes several public-domain hymns, such as *Amazing Grace*. You can add your own songs in the Library.

3. Choose an **arrangement**, the order the sections are sung in. You can click sections to remove them or add more for this service only.

4. Click **Add**.

</Steps>

The song is added as a **group**: one slide per two lines of lyrics (or four, depending on the theme), split by section. See [Songs](/design/songs/).

## 4. Add scripture

<Steps>

1. Click **Scripture** (<kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd>).

2. Type a reference, such as `john 3:16-18` or `ps 23`. The line under the field tells you what <kbd>Return</kbd> will do, for example "John 3:16–18 · ↵ adds 3 slides".

3. Press <kbd>Return</kbd> or click **Add**.

</Steps>

Each verse becomes one slide, grouped under the reference. See [Scripture](/design/scripture/).

## 5. Choose a look

Click **Presentation** above the slide list to show the presentation in the inspector on the right. Under **Theme**, click a theme to apply it to every slide. See [Themes](/design/themes/).

## 6. Choose your screens

<Steps>

1. Choose **Presentation › Screens…**.

2. Under **Audience screens**, turn on the projector or TV that should show the slides. Your own display is marked **Your screen** and can't be used.

   With only one display, turn on **Use an audience screen in a window** to show the slides in a window instead.

3. Click **Done**.

</Steps>

Guidepost remembers this setup by each screen's name and restores it next time the same screens are connected. For a TV over AirPlay, see [AirPlay TVs](/screens/airplay/).

## 7. Go live

<Steps>

1. Press <kbd>⌘2</kbd> to switch to Present.

2. Check the line under **GO LIVE**. It lists where the slides will go, for example "→ Epson Projector + Stage Display", and any problem that needs attention.

3. Click **GO LIVE**, or press <kbd>⌘Return</kbd>.

4. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>→</kbd> to move to the next slide, and <kbd>←</kbd> to go back. A presenter clicker works without any setup.

5. To finish, press <kbd>Esc</kbd> and confirm **End Live**.

</Steps>
