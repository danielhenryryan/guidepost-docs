Guidepost has a handful of ideas that the rest of this help builds on. They’re here in the order you’ll meet them, with links to the pages that have the steps.

## The Library and a presentation

The **Library** lives on this Mac and holds what you reuse from week to week: songs, media, themes, saved slides and Bibles. A **presentation** is the document for one service. It’s an ordered list of slides, saved as a single `.guidepost` file, and one presentation is open at a time.

What you add from the Library goes into the presentation, so the file has its own slides, themes, images and videos. You can build it on one Mac during the week and [open it on another](/files/sharing/) on Sunday.

This help says “presentation” for the file and “service” for what happens on Sunday.

## Slides, groups and songs

Every slide has a type: Text, Scripture, Song, Media or Blank. The type decides what the slide holds. A Scripture slide knows its passage and translation, and a Song slide knows its song and section. See [Adding slides](/design/adding-slides/).

Slides that belong together form a **group**. Guidepost makes a group when you add a song or a passage of several verses, and you can group any slides yourself. A group has a name and can be collapsed in the slide list. See [Slides and groups](/design/slides-and-groups/).

A song in the Library is made of sections, such as Verse 1, Chorus and Bridge. An arrangement is an order to sing those sections in. Every song has a **Default** arrangement and can have more, such as “Sunday short”. When you [add a song](/design/songs/) you choose an arrangement, and the whole song arrives as one group.

A finished slide you’ll want again, such as a welcome or offering slide, can be kept in the Library as a [saved slide](/design/saved-slides/).

## Themes and overrides

A **theme** is a look: fonts, colors, a background, text effects and layouts. The presentation has one theme and its slides follow it. A group can use a different theme, for example to give one song its own look.

A layout is where the text and media sit on a slide, for example **Lower third** or **Reference above**. Switching layouts keeps the text.

When you change something on one slide, such as its background, that slide overrides the theme for that one property. The inspector marks the property with a small dot and offers **Reset to theme**. Changing the theme later updates every slide that doesn’t override that property. See [Themes](/design/themes/).

A [background](/design/backgrounds/) can be a color, a gradient, an image, a video or one of the built-in backgrounds that come with Guidepost. **Dim** puts a tint over the background to keep the text readable.

## Going live

Nothing reaches a screen until you go live. **Go Live** starts showing the presentation on your screens and **End Live** stops it. While you’re live, Guidepost shows an amber **LIVE** badge. See [Going live](/present/going-live/).

Before you go live, the slide marked **Starts here** is the one that will be shown first. Once you’re live, Present has two monitors to watch. **Program** shows exactly what the audience screens show. **Up Next** shows the slide that **Next** will show.

## What each screen shows

An audience screen is a projector, TV or window that shows the slides to the congregation. You can have more than one, and they all show the same thing.

The [Stage Display](/screens/stage-display/) is a screen for the band and the speaker. It shows the current and next lyrics, a clock, the speaker timer and stage messages, none of which the congregation sees.

[Network viewers](/screens/network-viewers/) are phones, tablets and TVs on the church network that watch in a web browser. They see what the audience screens show.

You choose all of these in the [Screens panel](/screens/screens-panel/). When you aren’t live, network viewers and an audience screen in a window show the idle screen, such as your logo.

## Blank, Logo, Freeze and Clear text

Four buttons in Present temporarily change what the audience sees without losing your place. **Blank** shows a black screen. **Logo** shows your church logo. **Freeze** keeps the current slide up while you move ahead privately. **Clear text** hides the text and leaves the background.

Only one is active at a time, and pressing it again returns to the slide. See [Blank, Logo, Freeze and Clear text](/present/holds/).

## Messages to the audience and to the stage

A [message](/present/messages/) is a short line of text along the bottom of the audience screens, over the slide, such as “Please silence your phones”.

A [stage message](/present/cues/) is private. It goes to the speaker or the band on the Stage Display, for example “5 min” or “Wrap up”, and never appears on the audience screens.

The two [timers](/present/timers/) are split the same way. The countdown to the start of the service is shown on the audience screens. The speaker timer is shown only on the Stage Display.
