## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
- macOS 26 or later.
- An internet connection to download the app, activate a license and check for updates. You can prepare and run services offline.

Guidepost is a Mac app only. There is no Windows or Linux version.

## Install

<Steps>

1. Download Guidepost from the [Guidepost website](https://guidepostpresenter.com/beta).

2. Open the downloaded disk image and drag **Guidepost** into the **Applications** folder.

3. Open Guidepost from Applications or Launchpad.

   Guidepost is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a security warning.

</Steps>

Guidepost checks for updates automatically. See [Updates](/reference/updates/).

## The Start screen

When no presentation is open, Guidepost shows the Start screen.

- **New Presentation** creates an empty presentation and opens it in Design.
- **Open…** opens a presentation file from Finder.
- **Continue where you left off** shows the presentation you used last, with its first slide. Click **Continue** to open it.
- **Recent** lists other recent presentations, with a search field. Right-click a presentation to **Pin** it to the top of the list, **Show in Finder**, or **Remove from Recent**. Removing a presentation from the list doesn't delete the file.
- The **verse of the day** appears at the bottom, in your default translation.

### First launch

The first time you open Guidepost, the Start screen also shows:

- **Try the sample service**: a complete Sunday service with songs, scripture and announcements. Click **Open sample** to explore it. The sample opens as an untitled copy, so your changes don't affect the original. You can open it again at any time from **Help › Try the Sample Service**.
- **Get started**: a five-step checklist (Create a presentation, Add a song, Add scripture, Choose your screens, Go Live). Click a step to do it. Steps are checked off when you complete them anywhere in the app. Click **Hide** to hide the checklist permanently.

### What opens at launch

By default, Guidepost reopens the presentation you had open last. To see the Start screen every time instead, open **Settings › General** and set **When Guidepost opens** to **Show Start screen**. Double-clicking a presentation in Finder always opens that presentation.
