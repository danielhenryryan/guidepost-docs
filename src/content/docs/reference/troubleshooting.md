## Screens

### The projector shows my desktop before the service

Full-screen audience screens open when you go live or start a countdown. Until then, an extended display shows whatever macOS puts there, usually the desktop background. Start the [countdown](/present/timers/) to cover it before the service.

### My display isn't listed in the Screens panel

- Check the cable and that the display is on and set to the right input.
- Open **System Settings › Displays** and confirm that macOS sees the display.
- Your own display, the one the Guidepost window is on, is listed as **Your screen** and can't be a full-screen audience screen. To use a single display, turn on **Windowed audience screen**.

### The slides went to the wrong display

Open the [Screens panel](/screens/screens-panel/) and turn on the right display, or click **Make main**. While live, changes apply immediately.

### A display disconnected during the service

The service keeps going on the other screens. Reconnect the display: Guidepost puts the output back automatically. Or click **Choose screen** in the banner to use another display.

## AirPlay

### The TV shows my whole screen

The TV is mirroring your display. In **Control Center › Screen Mirroring**, choose **Use as Extended Display** under the TV. See [AirPlay TVs](/screens/airplay/#if-the-tv-is-mirroring-your-screen).

### The TV doesn't appear in Screen Mirroring

- Make sure the Mac and the TV are on the same network, and not a guest network.
- Turn the TV, or its Apple TV, on and wake it.
- Check that AirPlay is turned on in the TV's settings.

### The picture stutters or drops

AirPlay depends on the network. Connect the TV or Apple TV with an Ethernet cable if you can, and keep the Mac close to the Wi-Fi access point. For video-heavy services, a cable to the display is the most reliable option.

### Video sound plays on the TV instead of the sound system

In the Screens panel, set **Video sound** for the TV to **This Mac / sound system**. See [Video sound](/screens/airplay/#video-sound).

## Network viewers

### Phones can't connect

Work through the checklist in [If viewers can't connect](/screens/network-viewers/#if-viewers-cant-connect): same network, firewall, Local Network permission, and client isolation on the router.

### Viewers see a still picture during videos

Network viewers show video slides as a still frame. The audience screens play the video normally.

## Media

### A slide says "Missing: …"

The image or video file can't be found. Click **Replace…** to choose the file again, or **Remove**. See [Missing media](/design/media/#missing-media).

### A video won't import

Guidepost converts WebM, MKV, AVI and similar formats automatically. If conversion is off, turn on **Convert videos the Mac can't play natively** in **Settings › Library**, or export the video as MP4 or MOV and import that.

### A video stutters

Very large or very high-resolution videos may not play smoothly on older Macs. Export a smaller version, for example 1080p H.264 or HEVC, and replace the original.

## Files

### A presentation looks different on another Mac

If it uses a font the other Mac doesn't have, Guidepost shows a similar font until that font is installed. Install the font on that Mac, or use one of the fonts that come with Guidepost. See [Moving a presentation to another Mac](/files/sharing/).

### A presentation won't open

Guidepost explains why. If it was saved with a newer version of Guidepost, update this Mac. If it's on a drive that isn't connected, reconnect it.

### Guidepost asked to recover unsaved work

Guidepost quit unexpectedly with unsaved changes. Choose **Recover** to open them. See [Recovering after a crash](/files/saving/#recovering-after-a-crash).

## Keys

### Space or the arrow keys don't move slides

- Navigation keys work only in **Present**. In Design, arrow keys move through slides in the list or nudge layers.
- They don't work while you're typing in a field, such as the message composer. Press <kbd>Return</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to leave the field.
- Check whether the keys were changed in **Settings › Shortcuts**.

### B, L, F or C don't do anything

Holds are available only while live. Logo also needs a logo image in **Settings › Screens**.

## Still stuck?

Choose **Help › Report a Problem…** to send a report, or see [Getting help](/reference/support/).
