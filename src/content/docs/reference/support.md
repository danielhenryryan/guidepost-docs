## Report a problem

If something isn't working, choose **Help › Report a Problem…**.

1. Describe what happened, and what you expected.
2. Optionally, add your email address if you'd like a reply.
3. Leave **Include diagnostic details** selected to help find the cause. You can review the details before sending: the Guidepost and macOS versions, your Mac model, your display setup, and Guidepost's recent activity log. Your presentations and Library aren't included.
4. Click **Send report**.

You'll see a reference number when the report is sent. If it can't be sent, for example because you're offline, your description is kept so you can try again later.

## Send feedback

Choose **Help › Send Feedback…** to share an idea or tell us how Guidepost is working for your church. During the beta, Guidepost occasionally asks how it's going after a service. Click **Don't ask again** to stop the reminder.

## Contact support

Choose **Help › Contact Support**, or visit the [support page](https://guidepostpresenter.com/support).

## In the app

- **Help › Keyboard Shortcuts** lists every shortcut.
- **Help › Connecting an AirPlay Display** walks through connecting a TV.
- **Help › Silencing Notifications While Live** sets up Do Not Disturb during services.
- **Help › Adding Bible Translations** explains the supported formats.
- **Help › Try the Sample Service** opens a complete example service.
- **Help › What's New** shows recent changes.

You can also search the **Help** menu for a topic.
