## The open beta

Guidepost is in open beta and free to use, with every feature, through **December 31, 2026**. A **Beta** badge appears in the title bar.

- In the last week of the beta, a banner reminds you when it ends.
- From **January 1 to January 7, 2027**, everything keeps working, and Guidepost reminds you each time it opens.
- From **January 8, 2027**, editing, saving and the Library keep working without a license. Going live, the Stage Display, network viewers and the countdown need a license.

License changes take effect after you end live, so they won’t interrupt a service.

<Aside>
You can open, edit and save presentations and Library items without a license.
</Aside>

## Buy a license

A **Standard** license covers two Macs and is a one-time purchase. Click **Buy a license** in **Settings › License & About**, or visit the [pricing page](https://guidepostpresenter.com/pricing). Your license key arrives by email.

## Activate a license

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings › License & About**, or click the badge in the title bar.

2. Click **Enter license key**.

3. Paste the key from your purchase email and click **Activate**.

</Steps>

Activation needs an internet connection once. After that, Guidepost works offline indefinitely. It checks the license now and then when you're online. If it hasn't been able to check for a long time, a reminder appears. You can still run services.

### If activation doesn't work

Guidepost explains what went wrong and what to do:

- **That isn't a Guidepost license key**: check that you copied the whole key from the email.
- **All 2 computers on this license are in use**: deactivate Guidepost on one of the other Macs first (see below), or [contact support](/reference/support/).
- **Guidepost can't reach the license server**: check the internet connection and try again.

## Check your license

**Settings › License & About** shows your license status, how many of its computers are in use, the last four characters of the key, and when it was last checked. Click **Refresh license** to check it now.

## Move a license to another Mac

<Steps>

1. On the Mac you no longer use for Guidepost, open **Settings › License & About** and click **Deactivate this computer…**. Confirm **Deactivate**.

2. On the new Mac, activate the license with your key.

</Steps>

If the old Mac isn't available any more, [contact support](/reference/support/).
