title: Updates
description: Install updates, check for a new version and read release notes.
---

Guidepost checks for updates in the background and downloads them for you. An update installs when you quit Guidepost or click **Relaunch now**, never in the middle of a service.

## When an update is ready

A banner says, for example, "Guidepost 1.1 is ready. It installs when you relaunch, never during a service."

- **Relaunch now** installs the update and reopens Guidepost.
- **What's New** shows what changed.
- **Later** dismisses the banner. The update installs the next time you quit.

Update notices appear after you end live.

## Check for updates yourself

Choose **Guidepost › Check for Updates…**, or click **Check for app updates** in **Settings › License & About**. Guidepost tells you whether you're up to date.

To stop automatic checks, turn off **Check for updates automatically** in **Settings › General**.

## What's New

Choose **Help › What's New** to see the changes in the version you're using.

## If an update needs a newer macOS

Occasionally a new version of Guidepost needs a newer version of macOS. Guidepost tells you when that's the case, and keeps working on the version you have.
