Open Settings with <kbd>⌘,</kbd>, the gear in the title bar, or **Guidepost › Settings…**. Changes apply immediately. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close.

To find a setting, type in **Search settings** at the top of the sidebar. You can also find settings with [Search](/reference/search/) (<kbd>⌘K</kbd>) in the main window.

## General

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Look** | System, Light or Dark, for Guidepost's own window. Audience screens and the Stage Display aren't affected. |
| **Interface text size** | Default, Large or Larger, for Guidepost's controls. Slide text is set by themes. |
| **When Guidepost opens** | **Show Start screen** or **Reopen last presentation** (the default). |
| **Keep this Mac awake** | Prevents sleep and screen idling while live. On by default. |
| **Silence notifications** | Turns on Do Not Disturb while live. Needs a one-time setup; see [Silencing notifications](/reference/mac-integration/#silencing-notifications-while-live). |
| **Confirm before sending a cue** | When on, clicking a cue chip fills in the text and you click **Send**. When off, one click sends it, with Undo. |
| **Check for updates automatically** | On by default. See [Updates](/reference/updates/). |

Reduce motion, Increase contrast and Reduce transparency follow **System Settings › Accessibility**.

## Screens

The [Screens panel](/screens/screens-panel/), plus:

- **Logo**: the image for the Logo hold and the idle screen, and its background color.
- **When nothing is live, audience screens show**: Logo, Black or Wordmark.
- **Default transition**: the crossfade length in milliseconds. Themes and slides can override it.
- **Ask before going live with no audience screen**.

## Countdown

- **Duration** and **At zero** (Go Live, Hold at 0:00, Show Logo): the defaults for the [countdown](/present/timers/).
- **Text color** and **Background**: by default, these come from the presentation's theme. A preview shows the result.
- **Announcement loop**: whether saved slides rotate beside the countdown, how often they change, and which slides to use by default.

## Stage Display

Choose what appears on the [Stage Display](/screens/stage-display/#settings), adjust its appearance, and manage saved cues and speaker timer presets.

## Scripture & Songs

- **Bibles**: installed translations, the default translation, **Add translation…** and **Remove**. See [Bibles and translations](/library/bibles/).
- **First slide**: **Verse** or **Passage title card**, the default for new passages.
- **Long verses**: **Shrink to fit** or **Split across slides**.
- **Smallest text size**: the smallest size a long verse is shrunk to.
- **Lines per slide** for songs: 2 or 4.
- **Copyright display**: see [Copyright and CCLI](/reference/copyright/).

## Library

- Where the Library is stored, how much space it uses, and **Show in Finder**.
- **Recently deleted**, with **Empty now…**.
- **Convert videos the Mac can't play natively**: converts WebM, MKV, AVI and similar files in the background. On by default.
- **Warn about large files**: Over 500 MB, 1 GB (the default), 2 GB or 4 GB, or Never.
- **Offer to add media from presentations made on other Macs**.

## Network

The status and address for [network viewers](/screens/network-viewers/), help if viewers can't connect, and advanced options: **Port**, **Start when Guidepost opens** and **Turn off when I end live**. The on/off switch is in the Screens panel.

## Shortcuts

Every action and its keys, grouped by Everywhere, Present and Design. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/#change-a-shortcut).

## License & About

- **License**: your license status, with **Enter license key** and **Buy a license**, or, once licensed, **Refresh license** and **Deactivate this computer…**. See [Licensing and the beta](/reference/licensing/).
- **About**: the version, **What's New** and **Check for app updates**.
- **Help and support**: links to this help, keyboard shortcuts, AirPlay help, the sample service, support, and feedback.
