title: Adding slides
description: The Add row, slide kinds, layouts, and where new slides are placed.
---

You can add slides from the slide list, menus, Search, the Library or Present. The pickers and placement rules are the same in each.

## The Add row

Above the slide list, the **+ Add slide** row has four cards and a **More…** button:

| Card | What it adds | Shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | A Text slide in the theme's default layout. Click the small arrow on the card to start with **Title**, **Body**, **Quote**, **Lower third** or **Announcement**. | <kbd>⇧⌘T</kbd> |
| **Scripture** | Opens the Scripture picker. See [Scripture](/design/scripture/). | <kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd> |
| **Song** | Opens the Song picker. See [Songs](/design/songs/). | <kbd>⇧⌘W</kbd> |
| **Media** | Opens the Media picker and adds one Media slide per image or video. See [Media slides](/design/media/). | <kbd>⇧⌘M</kbd> |

**More…** offers:

- **Blank**: a slide with only a background (<kbd>⇧⌘N</kbd>).
- **Saved slides…**: copy in a slide from your Library. See [Saved slides](/design/saved-slides/).
- **Import slides from another presentation…**: choose a presentation file, choose the slides you want, and click **Add N slides**. If those slides use media your Library doesn't have, Guidepost offers to add it.
- **Slides from image…**: make Text slides from a photo or screenshot, such as a printed bulletin. Guidepost reads the text on your Mac. Adjust where slides split, then click **Add N Text slides**.

The same options are in **Presentation › Add Slide**. To add a Text slide at a particular spot, hover between two slides in the list and click **+**.

## Where new slides go

- New slides go **after the selected slide**. If the selected slide is in a group, they go after the whole group.
- With nothing selected, they go at the end.
- The first new slide is selected.
- Adding slides can be undone in one step with **Undo**.

In Present, slides added during a service go after the live slide's group. See [Changing the service while live](/present/live-changes/).

New slides use the presentation's theme, or the group's theme if they're added inside a themed group, unless you choose a different theme in the picker.

## Slide kinds

| Kind | What it holds | Built-in layouts |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | Up to three lines of text: **Heading**, **Body** and **Footer**. | Title (Centered, Left, Bottom), Body (Centered, Top, Bottom), Quote (Centered, Left), Lower third, Announcement |
| **Scripture** | One verse, with its passage and translation. | Reference below, Reference above, Reference hidden, Title card |
| **Song** | One part of a song section. | Lyrics only, Lyrics + section label |
| **Media** | One image or video. | Full screen, Framed |
| **Blank** | Only a background. | — |

## Changing a slide's layout

Select a slide and choose a layout under **Look › Layout** in the inspector. The text stays the same when you switch. A Text slide keeps all three of its lines even if the new layout shows fewer, so the text reappears when you switch back.

If you move or resize layers on the canvas, the layout shows as **Custom**. Click **Reset layout** to go back to the layout's positions.

## Editing song and scripture text

You can change the words on a Scripture or Song slide in the inspector or directly on the canvas. The slide is then marked **Edited**. Click **Revert to source** to go back to the original text from the Bible or the song.
