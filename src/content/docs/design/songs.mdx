The Song picker adds a song from your Library as a group of slides. It opens from the **Song** card in the Add row (<kbd>⇧⌘W</kbd>), from Search, and from Present with <kbd>W</kbd>.

## Find a song

Type in **Search songs**. The search covers titles, authors, lyrics, CCLI song numbers and tags, and each result says why it matched, for example "Matches lyrics: …". Use **Favorites**, **Recent** and **Tag** to narrow the list.

If the song isn't in your Library yet, click **New song…** to write it, or **Import…** to bring in song files. See [Songs and the song editor](/library/songs/) and [Importing songs](/library/importing-songs/).

## Choose an arrangement

An arrangement is the order the sections are sung in. Choose **Default** or one of the song's saved arrangements. The sections appear as chips in order.

To adjust it for this service:

- Click the **×** on a chip to remove that section.
- Drag chips to reorder them.
- Click a section under **Add a section** to add it to the end. A section can appear more than once.

Adjustments apply only to what you're adding. To keep them for next time, click **Update "Sunday short"** to change the arrangement you started from, or **Save as new…** to save a new arrangement.

## Choose how it looks

- **Theme:** keep the presentation's theme, or click another to give this song its own look.
- **Slides:** a preview of how the song will split into slides with the chosen theme.

## Add it

The footer summarizes what will be added, for example "Adding Amazing Grace · Sunday short, adjusted · 6 sections · 9 slides · copyright line on first slide". Click **Add**.

The song becomes one [group](/design/slides-and-groups/) named after the song. Slides are split:

- by section, so a slide never mixes the end of one verse with the start of the chorus;
- by the theme's **lines per slide** (2 or 4);
- wherever the song has a blank line inside a section, which forces a new slide.

A copyright line is added according to **Settings › Scripture & Songs › Copyright display**. See [Copyright and CCLI](/reference/copyright/).

<Aside>
While live, the picker's buttons are **Add & Show** and **Add**. See [Changing the service while live](/present/live-changes/).
</Aside>

## Editing lyrics on a slide

Select a Song slide to see its **Song**, **Section** and **Lines** in the inspector. Changing the lines here or on the canvas changes only this slide, which is marked **Edited**. Click **Revert to source** to go back to the song's lyrics. To change the Library copy, edit it in the song editor. Presentations that already use the song keep their existing lyrics; add the song again to use the updated version.

## Repeating or skipping a section during the service

You don't need to change the arrangement when the band repeats a chorus. In Present, the song map has **Repeat** and **Skip** for each section, which change only this run of the service. See [Changing the service while live](/present/live-changes/#repeat-or-skip-a-song-section).
