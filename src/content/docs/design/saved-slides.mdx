A saved slide is a finished slide kept in your Library, such as "Welcome", "Offering" or "Kids Church dismissal". Adding one copies it into the presentation, so editing the copy never changes the original.

## Save a slide

1. Select the slide.
2. Click **Save as Saved slide…** at the bottom of the inspector, right-click the slide and choose **Save as Saved Slide…**, or choose **Presentation › Save as Saved Slide…**.
3. Give it a name and, optionally, tags. Click **Save slide**.

Any images or videos the slide uses are saved with it.

## Add a saved slide

- In the Add row, click **More…** and choose **Saved slides…**. Choose a slide and click **Add slide**.
- Or open the Library drawer's **Saved** tab and double-click a slide, or drag it into the slide list.
- Or search for it with <kbd>⌘K</kbd>.
- Or, in **Library › Saved slides**, select it and click **Add to presentation**.

## Organize saved slides

In **Library › Saved slides** you can rename, tag, favorite, export, import and delete saved slides. Export a saved slide to use it on another Mac. See [Organizing the Library](/library/organizing/).

Saved slides can also rotate beside the countdown before a service. See [Countdown](/present/timers/#announcement-loop).
