The slide list on the left of Design shows every slide in order.

## Reading the slide list

- A slide on its own appears as a numbered thumbnail.
- A **group** appears as one thumbnail with its range (for example, "3–8"), a caption row with the group's name and a summary, and, when expanded, a compact row for each slide.
  - Song groups show the number of sections and slides, such as "6 sections · 9 slides".
  - Scripture groups show the reference and the number of verses. The translation is shown when it isn't your default.
- Compact rows show each slide's label (such as "Verse 1", "Chorus" or a verse number like "16a"), the start of its text, and its slide number.
- Badges show when a slide’s text has been split or edited:
  - **Split**: a long verse was split across more than one slide.
  - **Edited**: the slide's song or scripture text was changed from the original.
- **Live** marks the slide that's on screen during a service.
- **Skipped** slides are dimmed. See [Skipping slides](#skipping-slides).

Click a group's caption row to collapse or expand it.

## Selecting slides

- Click a slide to select it.
- <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click to select a range, or <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click to add or remove one slide.
- Use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to move through slides; add <kbd>⇧</kbd> to extend the selection.

Selected slides can be moved, duplicated, deleted, grouped or restyled together.

## Reordering

Drag slides, or a whole group by its thumbnail, to a new position. A line shows where they'll land. You can drag slides into a group, out of it, or within it. Groups can't be placed inside other groups.

## Duplicating, deleting and copying

With the slide list focused:

| Action | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Duplicate | <kbd>⌘D</kbd> |
| Delete | <kbd>Delete</kbd> |
| Cut, copy, paste | <kbd>⌘X</kbd>, <kbd>⌘C</kbd>, <kbd>⌘V</kbd> |
| Select all | <kbd>⌘A</kbd> |

Slides can be copied from one presentation and pasted into another. Every change can be undone with <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>.

## Working with groups

- **Make a group:** select slides and choose **Edit › Group** (<kbd>⌘G</kbd>). Type a name for the group and press <kbd>Return</kbd>.
- **Rename a group:** double-click its caption row, or choose **Rename Group…** from the right-click menu.
- **Ungroup:** select the group and choose **Edit › Ungroup** (<kbd>⇧⌘G</kbd>). The slides stay in place as individual slides.
- **Give a group its own theme:** right-click it, choose **Apply Theme to Group**, and pick a theme. Choose **Use Presentation Theme** to go back. A notice says how many slides changed.

## Skipping slides

To keep a slide in the file but pass over it during the service, right-click it and choose **Skip in Present**. Skipped slides are dimmed and marked **Skipped**. **Next** and **Previous** pass over them in Present, but you can still show one by clicking it. Choose **Skip in Present** again to turn it back on.

## The right-click menu

Right-click a slide for:

- **Duplicate**, **Delete**
- **Group**, **Ungroup**, **Rename Group…**, **Apply Theme to Group**
- **Set as Anchor Passage** (scripture groups only; see [Anchor passages](/present/navigation/#anchor-passages))
- **Copy Style**, **Paste Style**
- **Replace Media…** (when the slide has an image or video)
- **Save as Saved Slide…**
- **Skip in Present**

## Copying a slide's look

**Copy Style** (<kbd>⌥⌘C</kbd>) copies a slide's look, and **Paste Style** (<kbd>⌥⌘V</kbd>) applies it to the selected slides. With the canvas focused instead, the same commands copy and paste a layer's style.

## Slide notes

Every slide has **Notes** at the bottom of the inspector, for reminders such as "Wait for the pastor's cue". Notes appear in Present under **Up Next** and, if you choose, on the Stage Display. They're never shown to the audience.
