A Media slide shows one image or video, full screen or framed. To use an image or video *behind* text instead, set it as the slide's [background](/design/backgrounds/). To place one alongside other content, add it as a [layer](/design/canvas/).

## Add media slides

- Click **Media** in the Add row (<kbd>⇧⌘M</kbd>) to open the Media picker. Choose one or more items and click **Add slide** (or **Add 3 slides**). Each item becomes its own slide.
- Or drag image and video files from Finder, or items from the Photos app, onto the slide list.

### The Media picker

- **Library** shows the media already in your Library. Search by name or tag, filter with **Favorites**, **Recent**, tags, or the type menu (**Images**, **Videos**, **All media**). Double-click an item to choose it straight away.
- **From this Mac** adds new files: drop files or folders onto it, click **Choose files…**, or click **Add from Photos…**.

Media added from either tab is kept in your Library for next time.

## Fit

Select a Media slide and choose **Fit** in the inspector:

- **Cover** fills the screen, trimming the edges if the shape doesn't match.
- **Contain** shows the whole image or video, with the background around it.
- **Fill** stretches it to the screen's shape.

## Video playback

For a video, the inspector also shows how it plays during the service:

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **When the slide goes live** | **Play automatically**, or **Wait for Play**, so you start it from Present |
| **Loop** | On or off |
| **Sound** | On or off |
| **When it ends** | **Hold last frame**, **Show background**, or **Advance to next slide** |
| **Speed** | 0.5× to 2× |

See [Video during a service](/present/video/) for the controls in Present.

## Replace media

To swap an image or video for another one, right-click the slide and choose **Replace Media…**, or click **Replace…** in the inspector or the canvas toolbar. If the same item is used on other slides, choose **This slide only** or **Everywhere it's used**.

## Missing media

Saved presentations include copies of their media. If a media file is missing, the slide shows "Missing: sunrise.mp4" and a bar below the canvas offers **Replace…** and **Remove**.

Before going live, Guidepost warns you about missing media and lists the slides affected, with **Fix now** and **Go Live anyway**.

## Supported formats

- **Images:** JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, GIF and TIFF.
- **Video:** MOV, MP4 and M4V, including H.264, HEVC, ProRes, HDR, and video with a transparent background.
- **Converted automatically:** WebM, MKV, AVI, WMV, FLV and OGV videos are converted in the background when you import them. Progress shows in the title bar.

Audio files can't be added yet. See [Media in the Library](/library/media/) for more on importing.
