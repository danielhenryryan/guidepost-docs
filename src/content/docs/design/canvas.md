---
title: Editing on the canvas
description: Select, move, resize and rotate layers, edit text, and use guides, the grid and zoom.
---

The canvas shows the selected slide. Everything on it is a **layer**: a text box, image, video or shape. You can edit layers on any slide, including Song and Scripture slides. Those slides keep their song or passage information when you edit them.

## Add a layer

Use the buttons at the left of the canvas toolbar, the **Insert** menu, or right-click an empty part of the canvas:

- **Text Layer** (or press <kbd>T</kbd> with the canvas focused)
- **Image Layer…**
- **Video Layer…**
- **Shape Layer**, a rectangle filled with the theme's accent color

You can also drag an image or video onto the canvas from Finder, Photos or the Library drawer.

## Select

- Click a layer to select it. <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click to add or remove layers from the selection, or drag a box around several.
- Click an empty part of the canvas, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, to deselect.
- In the inspector's **Layers** list, hover a row to highlight that layer on the canvas, and click to select it. The list is the easiest way to reach a layer hidden behind another.

## Move, resize and rotate

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Move | Drag the layer. |
| Nudge | Press an arrow key to move 1 point, or <kbd>⇧</kbd>+arrow for 10 points. |
| Resize | Drag one of the eight handles. Hold <kbd>⇧</kbd> to keep the proportions. |
| Resize from the center | Hold <kbd>⌥</kbd> while resizing to keep the box centered: the opposite side moves by the same amount. |
| Resize text with its box | Hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> while resizing a text box to scale the font size with it. |
| Rotate | Drag the rotation handle above the layer. Hold <kbd>⇧</kbd> to snap to 15° steps. |
| Type exact values | Use **X**, **Y**, **W**, **H** and **Rotation** on the inspector's **Position** tab. |

## Edit text

Double-click a text layer, or press <kbd>Return</kbd> with it selected, to edit its text. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> when you're done. Text slides also have **Heading**, **Body** and **Footer** fields in the inspector, which stay in sync with the canvas.

With a text layer selected, the toolbar shows:

- font family and size, with a menu of preset sizes and **Fit text to box**;
- **Bold** (<kbd>⌘B</kbd>), **Italic** (<kbd>⌘I</kbd>) and **Underline** (<kbd>⌘U</kbd>);
- alignment and text color;
- **Fine type**: line height, letter spacing and case.

The inspector's **Style** tab sets the text effect: **None**, **Soft**, **Strong** (shadows) or **Outline**, each with its own settings.

## Images, videos and shapes

- **Image layers:** **Replace…**, opacity, and flip horizontally or vertically in the toolbar; fit, corner radius and blend mode in the inspector.
- **Video layers:** **Replace…** and **Mute** in the toolbar; playback options on the inspector's **Content** tab (the same options as [Media slides](/design/media/#video-playback)).
- **Shape layers:** fill, stroke color, stroke width and corner radius in the toolbar; a shadow in the inspector.

## Several layers at once

With two or more layers selected, the toolbar shows alignment (left, center, right, top, middle, bottom), and with three or more, **Distribute horizontally** and **Distribute vertically**. You can also change the font, text color and opacity of all selected layers together.

## Arrange, lock and hide

- **Edit › Arrange** has **Bring to Front** (<kbd>⌥⌘]</kbd>), **Bring Forward** (<kbd>⌘]</kbd>), **Send Backward** (<kbd>⌘[</kbd>) and **Send to Back** (<kbd>⌥⌘[</kbd>). You can also drag rows in the **Layers** list. The top row is in front.
- **Lock** a layer (<kbd>⇧⌘L</kbd>) so it can't be selected on the canvas by accident. Select it in the **Layers** list, or right-click it on the canvas and choose **Unlock**, to change it again.
- **Hide** a layer (<kbd>⇧⌘H</kbd>) to keep it on the slide without showing it.
- Double-click a row in the **Layers** list to rename the layer.

## Cut, copy and paste

Cut, copy, paste and duplicate (<kbd>⌘D</kbd>) work on layers when the canvas has focus. Pasting again offsets each copy slightly. Layers can be pasted onto another slide or into another presentation.

## Guides, grid and snapping

- **Smart guides** appear as you drag, snapping to the slide's edges and center, to other layers, and to equal spacing between layers. Hold <kbd>⌥</kbd> while dragging to move freely.
- **Rulers** (<kbd>⌘R</kbd>): drag from a ruler to make a guide, drag a guide back to the ruler to remove it, and double-click a guide to type its exact position. **View › Copy Guides to All Slides** repeats the current slide's guides on every slide.
- **Grid** (<kbd>⌘'</kbd>) shows a dot grid. Layers snap to it while it's showing.
- **Snap** (<kbd>⇧⌘;</kbd>) turns all snapping on or off. It's on by default.

## Zoom

Zoom ranges from 25% to 400%.

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Zoom in or out | <kbd>⌘=</kbd> or <kbd>⌘-</kbd>, <kbd>⌘</kbd>-scroll, or pinch on a trackpad |
| Fit the slide in the window | <kbd>⌘0</kbd> |
| Choose a level | Click the percentage at the right of the toolbar |

Zoom shortcuts work while the canvas has focus, and never zoom the whole window.
