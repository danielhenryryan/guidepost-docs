Design (<kbd>⌘1</kbd>) is where you build a presentation. It has four areas, left to right.

## Library drawer

A collapsible panel with your Library's **Songs**, **Media**, **Saved** slides and **Themes**, so you can add content without leaving Design. It's hidden at first. To show it, click the **Library** strip on the left edge, or choose **View › Show Library Drawer** (<kbd>⌥⌘L</kbd>).

- Double-click an item to add it. The line at the bottom says where it will go, for example "Adds after slide 4".
- Drag an item into the slide list to place it exactly, or drag an image or video onto the canvas to add it as a layer on the current slide.
- On the **Themes** tab, double-click a theme to apply it to the presentation, or to the selected slide's group.

## Slide list

The slides in order, with live thumbnails. Above them, the **+ Add slide** row adds new slides. See [Slides and groups](/design/slides-and-groups/) and [Adding slides](/design/adding-slides/).

## Canvas

The selected slide at full size, where you edit text and move, resize and rotate layers. The toolbar above the canvas changes with what's selected: with nothing selected it adds layers; with a text layer selected it shows font, size, style, alignment and color. See [Editing on the canvas](/design/canvas/).

## Inspector

The full record of whatever is selected:

- **Nothing selected:** the presentation's name, its **Theme** and its **Default transition**. Click **Presentation** above the slide list to get here.
- **A slide:** its **Content** (the text, passage, song section or media), its **Look** (layout, theme, background, transition) and its **Notes**. Buttons at the bottom save the slide as a [saved slide](/design/saved-slides/) or a [theme](/design/themes/).
- **A layer:** the list of layers on the slide, and tabs for the layer's **Content**, **Style** and **Position**.

Drag the thin lines between areas to resize them.

## Keyboard focus

Shortcuts act on whichever area has focus. When the slide list has focus, <kbd>Delete</kbd>, <kbd>⌘D</kbd>, copy and paste act on slides. When the canvas has focus, they act on layers. The focused area is outlined.
