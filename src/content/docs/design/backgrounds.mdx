A background sits behind a slide's text and layers. Each theme has a background that every slide uses unless you change it.

- To change one slide's background, select the slide and click **Change…** beside **Background** in the inspector.
- To change it for every slide, edit the theme's background. See [Themes](/design/themes/#edit-a-theme).

## Background types

| Tab | What you can set |
|---|---|
| **Color** | A solid color: the theme's colors, presets, or a hex value. |
| **Gradient** | Presets, or your own **Stops** (two or more colors) and **Angle**. |
| **Image** | An image from your Library. **Fit**: Cover, Contain or Fill. |
| **Video** | A video from your Library, with **Fit** and **Playback options**. Background videos are usually set to loop without sound. |
| **Scenes** | Animated and textured backgrounds built into Guidepost. |

## Scenes

Guidepost includes eight scenes: **Still Waters**, **Vesper**, **Crimson Veil**, **Linen**, **Slate**, **Dawn Haze**, **Ember** and **Aurora**. Hover over a scene to preview it, and click to apply it.

Scenes move gently on the canvas and on screens, and stay still in thumbnails. They pause while the screen is frozen, and when **Reduce motion** is on in **System Settings › Accessibility**.

## Dim

Turn on **Dim** to add a color tint over the background. This can make text easier to read over a bright image or video. Choose the **Dim color** and adjust its strength.

## Going back to the theme

When a slide has its own background, the inspector marks it with a dot and shows **Reset to theme**. Click it to use the theme's background again.
