The Library (<kbd>⌘3</kbd>) holds the content you reuse from week to week. Every presentation on your Mac can use it. You can use the Library with or without a presentation open.

## Layout

- **Sidebar:** the five sections (**Songs**, **Media**, **Saved slides**, **Themes** and **Bibles**), then filters for the current section: **Favorites**, **Recent** (used in the last 30 days) and your **Tags**. At the bottom, **Recently deleted**.
- **Middle:** the items in the section, with a search field, **Sort** (Name, Date added or Last used), and **List** or **Grid** view. Each section remembers its own view.
- **Right:** details and actions for the selected item.

The buttons at the top right change with the section: **Import…** and **New song** for songs, **Add from Photos…** and **Import…** for media, and so on.

## Adding to the presentation

Select one or more items and click **Add to presentation**, or right-click and choose **Add to Presentation**. The items are added after the selected slide in Design, following the same [placement rules](/design/adding-slides/#where-new-slides-go) as everywhere else.

You stay in the Library. A notice confirms what was added, for example "Added 'Amazing Grace' after slide 12", with **Show** to jump to it in Design.

You can also drag songs from the Library into Design's slide list, or use Design's [Library drawer](/design/workspace/#library-drawer).

## Sections

- **[Songs](/library/songs/)**: the bundled hymns and your own songs, in one list.
- **[Media](/library/media/)**: images and videos.
- **[Saved slides](/design/saved-slides/)**: finished slides to reuse.
- **Themes**: built-in themes and your own. Filter with **Built-in** and **Mine**. Select a theme to see its fonts, colors and background, and to **Apply to presentation**, **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Export** or **Delete…** it. See [Themes](/design/themes/).
- **[Bibles](/library/bibles/)**: the translations installed on this Mac.

## Keyboard

| To | Press |
|---|---|
| Move through items | Arrow keys |
| Extend the selection | <kbd>⇧</kbd> + arrow keys, or <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click |
| Add or remove an item from the selection | <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click |
| Edit a song, or rename media | <kbd>Return</kbd> |
| Preview | <kbd>Space</kbd> |
| Delete | <kbd>Delete</kbd> |
| Select all | <kbd>⌘A</kbd> |

## Where the Library is stored

**Settings › Library** shows where the Library is on your Mac, how much space it uses, and a **Show in Finder** button. Each saved presentation includes its media and themes, so you can open it on another Mac without copying the Library.
