In **Library › Songs**, click **Import…** to open **Import songs**. It has two tabs: **Files** and **Paste text**. The Song picker's **Import…** button opens the same window.

## Import song files

On the **Files** tab, drop files or folders onto the window, or click **Choose files…**. Folders are searched for song files, including any folders inside them.

Supported formats:

| Format | File extensions |
|---|---|
| ChordPro | `.cho`, `.chopro`, `.chordpro`, `.pro`, `.crd` |
| OpenLyrics | `.xml` |
| CCLI SongSelect | `.txt`, `.usr` |

Each file is imported as soon as it's read, and listed with its format, the number of sections, whether chords were kept, its CCLI number, and a status: **Imported**, **Replaced** or **Skipped**. Files that aren't songs are skipped with a reason.

A summary at the bottom totals the results, for example "Imported 12 songs · 1 duplicate skipped · 2 files skipped". Click **Show in Library** to see the new songs, or **Done**.

### Duplicates

A song with the same title as one already in your Library (and the same CCLI number, when both have one) is marked **Already in your Library**. Guidepost tells you when your copy was last edited and how many arrangements it has. Choose:

- **Skip**: keep your copy and ignore the file. This is the default.
- **Replace**: replace your copy with the file. Favorites and usage history are kept.
- **Keep both**: import the file as a separate song.

## Paste lyrics

On the **Paste text** tab, paste the lyrics of a song. Guidepost detects:

- the title, authors, CCLI number and key, if they're in the text;
- sections, from blank lines and labels such as "Verse 1" or "Chorus";
- chords written in square brackets.

Check the detected sections, change any label from its menu, then click **Add to Library**. You can fine-tune the song in the song editor afterwards.

## Read lyrics from a photo

To import lyrics from a printed song sheet or a screenshot, click **From image…** on the **Paste text** tab and choose the image, or drop an image onto the Files tab. Guidepost reads the text on your Mac and puts it in the paste box, where you can correct it before adding.
