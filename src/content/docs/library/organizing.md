## Favorites

Click the star beside a song, or choose **Add to Favorites** from the right-click menu, to mark it as a favorite. Turn on **Favorites** in the sidebar to show only favorites. The Song and Media pickers have a **Favorites** filter too.

## Tags

Tags group songs, media and saved slides across the Library, for example "Christmas", "Kids" or "Backgrounds". Tags appear in the sidebar with a count. Click one to show only items with that tag. Tags also filter the pickers in Design, and Search finds items by tag.

### Add tags

- **To one item:** in its details, click **+ Tag**, then type a tag name. Choose an existing tag, or **Create** a new one.
- **To several items:** select them, click **Tag…**, and check the tags to apply.
- **By dragging:** drag items onto a tag in the sidebar.

If you don't have any tags yet, click **Add starter tags** in the sidebar for a set of common ones: Worship, Hymns, Kids, Christmas, Easter, Communion, Backgrounds, Announcements and Video loops.

### Manage tags

Click **Manage** beside **Tags** in the sidebar. There you can:

- add a tag with **New tag**;
- rename a tag;
- change its color (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple or gray);
- delete it. If the tag is in use, Guidepost asks first. Deleting a tag doesn't delete the items.

## Search, sort and view

- Each section has its own search field. Songs can be searched by title, author, lyrics or CCLI number; media by name or tag.
- **Sort** by **Name**, **Date added** or **Last used**.
- Choose **List** or **Grid** view.

Each section remembers its own filters, sort and view.

## Delete and recover

Select items and press <kbd>Delete</kbd>, or click **Delete**. If an item is used in the open presentation, a saved slide, or presentations you've opened recently, Guidepost lists where and asks first. Items that aren't used anywhere are deleted right away, with **Undo** in the notice.

Deleting from the Library never breaks a saved presentation, because each presentation file contains its own copy of what it uses.

### Recently deleted

Deleted items stay in **Recently deleted**, at the bottom of the sidebar, for 30 days. Each shows how many days are left. Click **Restore** to bring one back.

To permanently delete everything in Recently deleted, click **Empty now…** and confirm. This can't be undone. You can also do this from **Settings › Library**.
