**Library › Songs** lists every song on your Mac in one alphabetical list: the hymns that come with Guidepost and your own songs.

## Bundled hymns

Guidepost includes six public-domain hymns, marked **Bundled**: *Amazing Grace*, *Be Thou My Vision*, *Come Thou Fount of Every Blessing*, *Holy, Holy, Holy*, *It Is Well With My Soul* and *Rock of Ages*.

You can edit a bundled hymn like any other song. Saving creates your own copy, marked **Your copy of a bundled hymn**, which keeps the hymn's favorite, tags and usage history. The original is hidden while your copy exists.

## Create or edit a song

- To write a new song, click **New song**.
- To edit a song, select it and click **Edit**, double-click it, or press <kbd>Return</kbd>.

The editor has three columns.

### Details

**Title** (required), **Authors** (separated by commas), **Copyright**, **CCLI song #**, **Key**, **Tempo** (in BPM), **Time** signature, and **Tags**.

The title, authors, copyright and CCLI number are used for the copyright line on slides. See [Copyright and CCLI](/reference/copyright/).

### Sections

Each section has a label, a number, and a box for its lyrics.

- Add a section with the buttons at the bottom: **Verse**, **Chorus**, **Pre-Chorus**, **Bridge**, **Tag**, **Intro**, **Outro**, or **Custom…** for any other label, such as "Vamp". **Interlude** and **Ending** are in each section's label menu.
- Numbers are assigned automatically. Type in the number field to change one.
- Drag sections to reorder them. Click the trash button to delete one.
- Each section shows how many slides it will make, such as "→ 2 slides".

A **blank line** inside a section's lyrics forces a new slide at that point.

### Chords

Write chords inline, in square brackets, right before the syllable they belong to:

```
[G]Amazing [C]grace, how [G]sweet the sound
```

Chords are never shown on audience screens. They appear above the lyrics on the [Stage Display](/screens/stage-display/#chords) when **Chords above lyrics** is turned on.

### Arrangements

An arrangement is the order the sections are sung in. Every song has a **Default** arrangement, and new sections are added to the end of it automatically.

- Click **+ New** to add a named arrangement, such as "Sunday short" or "Christmas".
- Select an arrangement to edit its order. Click **+ Section** to add a section to the end (a section can appear more than once), drag chips to reorder, and click a chip to remove it.
- Right-click a named arrangement to **Rename…** or **Delete** it.

You can also adjust an arrangement while adding a song, and save the change from the Song picker. See [Songs](/design/songs/#choose-an-arrangement).

### Slide preview

**Slides** shows how the song will split into slides with the current presentation's theme (or Clean, when no presentation is open), including forced breaks.

### Saving

Click **Save changes**. The header summarizes what changed, for example "Changed · details, 2 sections, 1 arrangement". If you click **Cancel** after making changes, Guidepost asks before discarding them.

Changing a song in the Library doesn't change presentations that already use it. To update a presentation, add the song again.

## Other song actions

Select a song to see its details, arrangements and sections on the right, and how often it's been used. From there, or by right-clicking:

- **Add to presentation**
- **Edit**
- **Duplicate**: makes a copy named "*Title* copy", with the same tags.
- **Export**: saves the song as a **ChordPro** or **OpenLyrics** file, to use in other apps or on another Mac.
- **Add to Favorites**
- **Delete**

Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to preview a song's lyrics without opening it.
